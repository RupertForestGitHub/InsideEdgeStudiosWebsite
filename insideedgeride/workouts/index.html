Workouts and smart-trainer control

Choose the purpose. Press play. Ride the workout.

Inside Edge Ride turns structured ZWO workouts into personalised targets for a compatible Bluetooth smart trainer. No virtual world, no distracting game—just the session, the rider and the information needed to execute it well.

01

Choose

Find a workout by the adaptation you want.

Browse a growing library organised by training zone. Descriptive names, duration, estimated load, intensity and miniature profiles make comparison quicker than guessing from an abstract workout title.

  • Sort by duration, estimated load or alphabetically
  • Workouts from recovery through anaerobic capacity
  • FTP test protocols kept in their own section
  • ZWO files translated into readable workout structure
02

Preview

See the shape of the work before committing to it.

Explore the workout graph interval by interval. Duration, watts, W/kg and percentage of FTP are calculated from the rider’s current profile, while the original structure remains visible and understandable.

03

Ride

Everything important stays visible under pressure.

Actual and target power, heart rate, cadence, workout time, target zone and the full moving profile share one landscape view. Skip, pause or adjust bias without losing sight of the current and next interval.

ERG or PassiveLet the trainer control power or ride freely while timing and recording continue.
Responsive controlsPause, change interval and adjust workout bias from the riding screen.
Expanded viewPrioritise the graph and headline metrics on iPhone or iPad.
04

Review and keep

Finish with a useful record—not a disappearing result.

The completed workout combines performance metrics with the actual ride graph. Save it locally in a readable history or export it for analysis and sharing elsewhere.

  • Ride time, distance and cadence
  • Average and maximum power
  • Average and maximum heart rate
  • Relative intensity and estimated load
  • Text, CSV, PDF and TCX export

Simple by design

A cost-effective route into structured indoor training.

Use the smart trainer and Apple device you already own. Inside Edge Ride focuses on training purpose, reliable control and understandable results without requiring access to a virtual cycling platform.
