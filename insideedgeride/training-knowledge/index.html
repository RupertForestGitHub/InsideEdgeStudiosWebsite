Offline by design

The library remains useful when the network does not.

The current workout collection is downloaded and cached locally. A bundled fallback means the app can still provide structured training when Dropbox or the network is unavailable.

The original ZWO can also be saved or shared, keeping the workout portable rather than locking it inside one service.
