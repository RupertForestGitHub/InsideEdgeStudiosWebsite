Workout Ride

A real workout. A real trainer. Three focused views.

Press play and let ERG mode apply each target using your FTP. Standard View combines controls, metrics, interval guidance and the profile; Focused View gives the live data more room; Max Metrics makes the essential numbers impossible to miss.

ControlAutomatic ERG targets, workout bias, pause and interval navigation.
RespondSwitch between ERG and Passive while timing and recording continue.
SeeCycle through Standard, Focused and Max Metrics views while the workout continues.

Free Ride

Ride freely—or ask the trainer to hold your target.

Start in Passive mode or select a target for ERG control. Live power, cadence, heart rate and W/kg remain visible while the rolling 120-second trace builds a colour-coded record of your effort.

Max Metrics View

Swipe through the ride. See every number at full scale.

Keep the essential workout targets in front of you, then move continuously through live power, ride averages and heart-rate performance. Large, high-contrast data cards stay readable when the effort gets hard.

Designed for the effort

Useful when looking at the screen is the last thing you want to do.

Large digital readouts and clearly separated controls make the screen easier to use during hard intervals. Cycle between Standard, Focused and Max Metrics views, then swipe through live workout, power, averages and heart-rate pages when the numbers deserve the whole display.

  • Live zone names and target-status guidance
  • One-touch pause, skip and workout bias controls
  • Real-time power, averages and heart-rate performance screens
  • Portrait and landscape layouts for iPhone and iPad
