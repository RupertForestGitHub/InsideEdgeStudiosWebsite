Learn — Adapt — Perform

Knowledge turns training data into purposeful riding.

Knowledge is one of a cyclist’s greatest assets. The more you understand your own training, the more purposeful and effective every ride can become.

Inside Edge Ride turns your FTP, weight and lactate-threshold heart rate into personalised Power, W/kg and Heart Rate training zones. Clear guidance explains what each zone means, why it matters and how to use it for indoor and outdoor cycling.

Put that knowledge into action with structured workouts from the included library—or create, customise and import your own. Connect a compatible smart trainer, follow live targets, record your performance and replay completed rides using captured power, heart rate and cadence data.

Whether you ride for fitness, performance or enjoyment, Inside Edge Ride provides the knowledge and practical tools to help you train with greater purpose.
