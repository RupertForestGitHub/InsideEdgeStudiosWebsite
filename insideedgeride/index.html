Your zones at a glance

Print your personalised training grid and keep it in front of you.

Inside Edge Ride turns your FTP, weight and lactate-threshold heart rate into a clear, colour-coded reference covering every training zone. Export the full grid as a PDF, print it and keep it beside your indoor setup for an immediate check of target watts, W/kg, heart rate and perceived effort while you ride.
