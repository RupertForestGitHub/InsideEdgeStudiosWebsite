More than a workout player

From first calculation to completed ride report.

Inside Edge Ride joins together tools that are often scattered across websites, spreadsheets and subscription platforms. Personal metrics feed the zone grid. Zones explain the training library. The library feeds Workout Ride. The completed session returns as a readable record.

Connect a compatible Bluetooth FTMS trainer and heart-rate monitor for live control and recording—or use Passive mode for a freeform effort. Save locally, then share text, CSV, PDF or TCX files with the completed workout graph, actual power, heart rate, relative intensity and estimated load.

FTP & LTHR→Personal zones→Workout choice→Trainer control→Ride report

Simple by design

Structured smart-trainer riding without an ongoing platform subscription.

There is no virtual world to join and no account required to understand your own data. Inside Edge Ride focuses on the workout, the rider and the equipment already available.

  • Downloadable and cached ZWO workout library
  • Automatic ERG targets with a Passive alternative
  • Personal metrics stored on the device
  • Local ride history with open export formats
