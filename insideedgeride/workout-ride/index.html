Information hierarchy

Everything needed during the effort—nothing that gets in the way.

01

Large live metrics

Actual and target power, interval time, real riding time, cadence, heart rate, W/kg, FTP percentage and current zone.

02

Planned and actual profile

The coloured workout shape stays visible with a fine dark-blue actual-power trace and heart-rate trace.

03

This and next interval

Duration, target watts and W/kg provide passive guidance that updates automatically at each transition.

04

Deliberate controls

Separated previous, pause and next controls reduce mistakes. Larger bias controls remain easy to use under pressure.

ERG mode

Let the trainer hold the target.

Inside Edge Ride translates the workout percentage and saved FTP snapshot into watts, then sends each target to a compatible FTMS trainer.

  • Smooth interval transitions
  • 50–150% workout bias
  • Timing continues through navigation and reconnection
  • Controls relinquished safely when the ride ends

Passive mode

Ride freely while everything records.

Turn ERG off for freeform riding, equipment limitations or sustained FTP tests. The workout clock, interval guidance and telemetry continue normally.

  • No prescribed resistance
  • Current and next targets remain visible
  • Power and heart-rate traces continue
  • Switch modes during a normal workout

Completed ride

Keep the ride—not just the memory of it.

Save a readable record locally and review average and maximum power, heart rate, cadence, riding time, workout progress, Relative Intensity and Estimated Load.

The completed graph combines the planned profile with actual power and heart rate, then appears in the app and PDF report.

Cost-effective training

Your compatible trainer can be useful without another monthly platform.

Inside Edge Ride is deliberately focused: understand the workout, control the trainer, record the effort and keep an open copy of the result.

Compatibility depends on the trainer advertising the relevant Bluetooth FTMS monitoring and target-power capabilities. Passive guidance remains available where ERG is not.
