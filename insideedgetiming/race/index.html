Inside Edge Timing · Race Timer

Finish-order recording for real event pressure.

Race Timer is built for the practical manual timing situations many clubs still rely on: prepare the event and start list, capture each finisher, assign athlete details, then save or share the result before the next race rolls around.

Race mode

From setup to an assigned finish order.

Prepare and record the race

Set the field before the start, then capture the line under pressure.

Identify the competition and date, then set race number, distance, round and category through clear summary tiles. An optional start list can be prepared before the clock begins. Tap Record Finisher at the line and the app preserves order, assigns placings and calculates total time and the gap to the winner.

After the finish, assign prepared entrants to their captured positions or correct details manually. A locally cached roster remains available when connectivity is poor, while validation warns about reused race numbers, matching saved races and blank finisher details.

  • Optional pre-race start list
  • Expanded live race view once timing begins
  • Assign entrants without losing captured times

Save, review and share

Keep the result useful after the clock has stopped.

Review the captured order and assign the prepared athletes before saving. Races are identified by competition, date, race number, distance, round and category; the app warns before replacing an exact match, then selects the next available race number for the event.

History keeps interval reports, split sessions and race results on the device. Share one record through iOS or export a wider competition report. No Inside Edge account is required.

  • Return to local history
  • Share one result or a competition group
  • Keep working without reliable internet

Race-day workflow

Built for the two-minute gap before the next race.

  1. 01

    Prepare the event and race

    Set the competition and date, then identify race number, distance, round and category. Summary tiles keep setup readable and flag event or race-number conflicts before timing begins.

  2. 02

    Build an optional start list

    Pre-populate the race with expected entrants before the start. The cached roster can complete skater details, while manual entry remains available.

  3. 03

    Start, capture and assign

    Start the expanded race clock and tap Record Finisher as each athlete crosses the line. Afterwards, assign prepared entrants to the captured positions without altering their times or order.

  4. 04

    Validate, save and move on

    The app warns about blank finishers and matching saved races. Save the result, move to the next available race number, then share one race or export the competition group.
